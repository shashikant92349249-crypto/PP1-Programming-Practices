**Program 5: Area of a Rectangle**



**Algorithm**



Step 1: Start.

Step 2: Input the length (L) and breadth (B) of the rectangle.

Step 3: Calculate the area using the formula:

Area = L × B

Step 4: Display the area of the rectangle.

Step 5: Stop.
