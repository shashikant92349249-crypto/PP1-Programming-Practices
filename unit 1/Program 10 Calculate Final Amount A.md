**Program 10: Calculate Final Amount After Discount**

**Algorithm**



Step 1: Start.

Step 2: Input quantity (Q), price per item (P) and discount percentage (D).

Step 3: Calculate the total amount.

Total = Q × P

Step 4: Calculate the discount amount.

Discount = (Total × D) / 100

Step 5: Calculate the final amount.

Final Amount = Total − Discount

Step 6: Display the total amount, discount amount and final amount.

Step 7: Stop.
