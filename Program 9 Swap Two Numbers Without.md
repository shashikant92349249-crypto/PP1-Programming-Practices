**Program 9: Swap Two Numbers Without Using a Third Variable**

**Algorithm**



Step 1: Start.

Step 2: Input two numbers a and b.

Step 3: Calculate a = a + b.

Step 4: Calculate b = a - b.

Step 5: Calculate a = a - b.

Step 6: Display the swapped values of a and b.

Step 7: Stop.
